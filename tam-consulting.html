Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TAM Consulting's services and how we work.

What accounting software do you support?
We are certified experts in QuickBooks and Zoho Books, and also support ERP systems. We can help you choose, set up, and train your team on the best solution for your business size and industry.
Do you provide remote bookkeeping services?
Yes — we offer full remote bookkeeping and accounting services for clients across Pakistan and internationally. All work is done securely via cloud accounting platforms.
Can you file sales tax and income tax returns?
Absolutely. We handle all FBR and SRB filings — including income tax, sales tax, and withholding tax returns. We ensure full compliance with the latest regulations.
Do you offer monthly accounting packages?
Yes. We offer flexible monthly retainer packages for bookkeeping, reporting, and tax compliance — tailored to the size and needs of your business. Contact us for a custom quote.
Can startups and new businesses use your services?
We love working with startups. We can help with business registration, NTN/STRN registration, initial accounting setup, payroll, and all foundational financial structures from day one.
Is my financial data kept confidential?
Client confidentiality is our top priority. All financial data is handled under strict non-disclosure agreements, with secure digital protocols and access controls.
